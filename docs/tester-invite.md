## Longer invite (email, forum or a group chat)

> **Help test Destiny Unbound (free, early alpha)**
>
> Destiny Unbound is a free, open-source fantasy character forge and story game. Every part of your hero (race,
> birthplace, class, powers, weapon, weakness and more) is decided by a spin of the wheel. Then you play out their
> saga, chapter by chapter, until they retire, ascend or fall.
>
> I'm looking for a handful of testers to find bugs and tell me what's confusing before more people play.
>
> **How to join**
> 1. Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/McDS5HVxzJ. When it asks "What brings you to Destiny Unbound?", pick
>    **Help test the alpha** and you get the **Tester** role. That opens the Testing channels: #bug-reports,
>    #suggestions, #feedback and #tester-chat (for chatting with the other testers).
> 2. Get the game: https://bzrkherja.itch.io/destiny-unbound. Play in your browser, or download the Windows
>    version. Mac and Linux builds are at https://github.com/ScikuVRC/destiny-unbound/releases (untested so far,
>    so reports from those are extra welcome).
>
> **Windows will warn you** the first time ("Windows protected your PC") because the alpha isn't code-signed yet.
> Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The code is open source if you want to check it.
>
> **What to do** (about 30 minutes): the checklist is pinned in #feedback on the Discord. In short, spin a legend,
> play its story to the end, try the arena and the Daily Challenge, and switch through the four themes.
>
> **Found something?** One post per bug in #bug-reports, or use the bug report form on GitHub if you prefer:
> https://github.com/ScikuVRC/destiny-unbound/issues/new/choose. Ideas go in #suggestions (or the suggestion
> form). In the game, Options > Data > Send feedback saves your error log and a screenshot you can attach.
>
> Thank you for helping!

## Checklist post (for #feedback on the Discord)

> **Alpha tester checklist** 🧪
> Thanks for testing! Post bugs in #bug-reports (one per post) and ideas in #suggestions. Chat with the other
> testers in #tester-chat. Prefer GitHub? https://github.com/ScikuVRC/destiny-unbound/issues/new/choose
>
> **Session 1 (about 30 minutes)**
> ☐ Start the game and read the four-step guide. Was anything unclear?
> ☐ Spin one legend by hand and one with Auto-spin
> ☐ Begin its story and play until it ends (death, retirement or ascension)
> ☐ Try the arena once and the Daily Challenge once
> ☐ Switch through all four themes. Does any screen look broken?
> ☐ Open Options: try display mode, window size, interface scale and the volume sliders
>
> **Session 2 (any time later)**
> ☐ Play with a controller if you have one
> ☐ Try Options > Accessibility: text size, high contrast, colour-blind-safe wheels, sound captions
> ☐ Save a legend to the Hall, export your save (Options > Data), then import it again
> ☐ Continue a legend's story as its heir
> ☐ Desktop app with Discord open: does your Discord status show what you're doing?
> ☐ Optional: try the mod kit from the itch.io page, or install a mod (Menu > Mods)
>
> **When you're done, tell us in #feedback:**
> 1. What was the best moment?
> 2. What was the most confusing moment?
> 3. Would you play again tomorrow? Why or why not?
