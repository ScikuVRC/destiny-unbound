# Mod gallery

Mods and wheel packs for Destiny Unbound: new themes, sounds and wheel entries. How to make one is in the
[mod kit guide](../modkit/GUIDE.md); this page is where finished ones are listed.

## How to install

- **A mod** (a folder with `mod.json`): in the desktop app, open **Content & mods > Packs and mods > Open mods
  folder**, copy the folder in and press **Restart to apply**. In a browser, use **Import a mod folder**.
- **A wheel pack** (one `.json` file): **Content & mods > Packs and mods > Import a pack file**.

Mods can't run code: the game checks every value when it loads one and refuses anything it doesn't allow.

## Share yours

1. Zip the mod's folder (or take the pack's `.json` file).
2. Post it where people can download it: itch.io, GitHub, Google Drive, anywhere.
3. Tell us with the [Share a mod form](https://github.com/ScikuVRC/destiny-unbound/issues/new?template=mod.yml) on
   GitHub, or post it in **#mods** on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/McDS5HVxzJ) (a forum: one post per mod, tagged
   Theme, Wheel pack, Sounds or Work in progress).

To be listed, a mod needs to:

- be your own work, or use only things whose licence allows sharing them (say which);
- keep settings original: names, factions and symbols from other games, books or films belong to their owners;
- work in the current version, and say which base theme it uses.

Your mod is yours: you choose its licence. Listing it here is a link, not a copy.
