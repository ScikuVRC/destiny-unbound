# Grok prompts: UI redesign mockups

For grok.com (Imagine). Generate by hand; credit any image we use "Created with Grok" and log it in
`art-credits.md`. Grok returns 1168x784 images, so each prompt asks for one sheet with four mockups in a 2x2 grid.

## Prompt 1: the spin screen, four directions

```
A UI design presentation sheet, 2x2 grid of four distinct, polished desktop game UI mockups (16:9 screens, thin
gaps between them, no device frames), for a dark-fantasy character-creation game called "Destiny Unbound" where
the player spins large ornate wheels of fate to roll a hero's race, powers and destiny. Every mockup shows the same
screen: a big circular wheel of fate made of many thin coloured slices with tiny engraved labels, a dagger-shaped
pointer at the top, a glowing bronze hub; a question title such as "WHAT'S YOUR RACE?"; a large glowing result word
such as "ARCHFEY"; a prominent SPIN button; small secondary buttons; and a side panel titled "YOUR LEGEND" listing
attributes (race, birthplace, age, class, strength, speed) with small green buff and red debuff tags and a power
rating bar.
Top-left mockup: "Ashen Crown" - black iron and ember orange, gothic engraved borders, the wheel on the left, the
panel on the right, warm candle glow.
Top-right mockup: "Astral Oracle" - deep navy and starlight gold, celestial constellation lines, the wheel centred
with the panel as a floating parchment card.
Bottom-left mockup: "Iron Litany" - grimdark sci-fi cathedral, gunmetal and blood red, angular plates and rivets,
holographic wheel, panel as a data-slate.
Bottom-right mockup: "Blood Moon" - gothic horror, crimson and bone white, a huge moon behind the wheel, panel as an
old ledger with wax seal.
Clear visual hierarchy: the wheel and SPIN button stand out first, the result second, the panel third. Generous
spacing, aligned columns, crisp readable serif typography, consistent icon style, no clutter. High-end game UI
concept art, dribbble / artstation UI showcase quality, flat presentation, sharp details, no people, no real
brand logos, no watermark.
```

## Prompt 2 (optional): four screens of one theme

```
A UI design presentation sheet, 2x2 grid of four polished desktop game UI mockups (16:9, thin gaps, no device
frames) for a dark-fantasy wheel-of-fate RPG called "Destiny Unbound", all in one consistent style: black iron,
ember orange and old gold, gothic engraved borders, warm candlelight glow, crisp serif typography.
Top-left: the main menu - large logo "DESTINY UNBOUND" top-left, a vertical menu (New Legend, Story, Arena, Daily
Challenge, Hall of Legends, Options) over a painted background of a giant stone wheel of fate in a ruined
cathedral.
Top-right: the spin screen - a large ornate wheel with many thin slices on the left, the question "WHAT'S YOUR
BIRTHPLACE?" and the result "FARMSTEAD" in the middle with a SPIN button, and a "YOUR LEGEND" attribute panel with
small green and red tags on the right.
Bottom-left: the finished character card - a tall framed trading-card style portrait slot, name and title, rank
badge, stat bars, power rating.
Bottom-right: the story screen - a chapter wheel with chapter names on the slices, "CHAPTER 5: WHAT HAPPENS
NEXT?", and a scrolling saga log of past chapters with small icons.
Clear hierarchy, generous spacing, aligned grids, readable at a glance, no clutter. High-end game UI concept art,
artstation UI showcase quality, flat presentation, no people, no real brand logos, no watermark.
```
