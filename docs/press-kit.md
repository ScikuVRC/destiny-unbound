## In one sentence

Spin the wheels of fate to forge a fantasy hero, then live their saga: make choices, fight bosses, found a
dynasty and reach one of ten endings, or die trying.

## About the game

Every part of your hero is a spin: race and bloodline, birthplace, age, class, strength, powers, weapon, companion,
weakness and title, over forty wheels in all. Bigger slices are likelier, so a human farmhand is common and a god
is a sliver; when a sliver hits, the wheel creeps over the last slices and lights up. Every result brings buffs and
debuffs that push the other wheels up or down.

A finished hero begins their story, chapter by chapter: quests, dragons, kingdoms, wars, rivals, love and heirs.
At key moments the player chooses and the wheel decides how it goes. Death is real, ten endings wait, and an heir
can carry the house on. Heroes who survived, or cheated death, can turn up again in later sagas.

## What makes it different

- **Honest odds.** Slice size is the real probability, and fights show their real chances on the wheel.
- **A saga, not just a character sheet.** 49 kinds of chapter, choices that matter, nemeses, kingdoms and
  dynasties, an epilogue and a saga recap at the end.
- **Made to share.** A short card code opens any hero in the game; a print sheet turns a hero into a real card.
- **Open.** A Wheel Workshop changes any wheel in the game, and mods add themes, sounds and new entries.
- **Accessible.** Full controller support, rebindable keys, colour-blind-safe wheels, high contrast, reduced
  motion, sound captions and screen reader support.

## How it was made, honestly

Destiny Unbound is made by one person with the help of AI tools, and says so:

- The painted art (title screens, portraits, chapter and ending paintings) was made with Grok and is credited
  "Created with Grok" in [art-credits.md](art-credits.md).
- The optional HD music is AI-generated (ACE-Step) and listed in [music-credits.md](music-credits.md). The game
  also has its own synthesized music, and the HD music can be switched off.
