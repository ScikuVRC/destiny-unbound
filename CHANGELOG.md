# Changelog

What changed in each release of Destiny Unbound. The release workflow uses the section for each version as its
GitHub release notes. Builds: [GitHub releases](https://github.com/ScikuVRC/destiny-unbound/releases) and
[itch.io](https://bzrkherja.itch.io/destiny-unbound).

## 0.3.0-alpha.10 (2026-11-23)

### New

- **Five new story arcs**, each running over three chapters:
  - **The hungry blade:** a whispering sword that grows stronger the more you feed it. Feed it or starve it, then
    break it, master it, or throw it into the sea.
  - **The war of the banners:** join the Silver Crown or the Ember Rebellion, fight the great battle, and win a land,
    a free country, or a price on your head.
  - **A sibling you never knew:** follow the trail of a lost twin and find out who they became.
  - **The grey fever:** help the sick, look for a cure or run, then live through the plague as it spreads.
  - **The Grand Circuit:** three tournaments in three cities, ending in a final before fifty thousand people.

## 0.3.0-alpha.9 (2026-11-23)

### New

- **Set bonuses.** Twelve sets of three pieces, like Child of the Flame (fire element, a fire weapon, fire in the
  blood) or Lord of the Dead. Two pieces give a named bonus, all three a bigger one; a note pops up while you spin.
- **Hall tournament.** A new Versus format: the eight strongest heroes in your Hall fight a seeded bracket with the
  real odds. The champion earns the title Champion of the Hall.
- **Monthly challenge.** One rule a month for everyone, like a Month of the Dead or a Month of Giants. Finish a saga
  with a hero made under it to earn the month's badge. Daily Challenge on the menu now offers both.
- **Daily streaks.** Days in a row of finished daily challenges, with a badge at 3, 7 and 30 days.
- **Milestones.** Nine long achievements in bronze, silver and gold, with progress bars.
- **Moving title screens.** The painted title art drifts slowly, with embers, stars, sparks, ash or fireflies to
  match each look (only with animations fully on).

### Changed

- **Screen readers.** The result is announced once instead of every slice the pointer passes, and the wheel says how
  many results it has and the likeliest ones.

## 0.3.0-alpha.8 (2026-11-23)

### New

- **Age that matters.** Heroes grow older through life stages that follow their race's lifespan: middle age, old
  age and a venerable old age each take a little from the body and give a little to the mind. Old heroes get four
  chapters of their own: pass on the sword, one last adventure, a last meeting with the old enemy, and the council of
  elders. Years help with study, mentors and dragons, and slow down training and heists. Ageless races never age, and
  all of it follows the Aging option.
- **Gold and a travelling merchant.** Treasure, heists, dragons, tournaments and conquests pay out gold, shown in the
  saga and the recap; an heir inherits half. Now and then a travelling merchant turns up between chapters with three
  wares: stat boosts, a healing draught that turns your next death into a narrow escape, a hired sellsword or a noble
  title. The merchant can be switched off in the story settings.

## 0.3.0-alpha.7 (2026-11-23)

### New

- **A portrait for every race.** All 98 races now have a painted portrait, so every hero's card has a picture.
- **Faces for friends and foes.** Companions have portraits by who they are (a mentor, a ghost, your royal in-laws, a
  young dragon...), and your nemesis has a villain's face of their own. They show on the saga sheet, in the recap and,
  for the nemesis, in the battle report.
- **A map of the saga.** The saga recap draws your hero's road across a painted world map, chapter by chapter, with
  the ending marked at the last stop.

### Changed

- **World Boss.** Four bosses take turns for now: Genesis, the Valkyr of Time, has been taken out of the rotation.
  This week's boss stays the same.

## 0.3.0-alpha.6 (2026-11-21)

### New

- **Versus, three ways.** One fight, best of three, or a team fight: three against three, winner stays on. Copy a
  challenge sends a friend your hero's Legend Code (Versus reads it straight out of the message), and you can save a
  result card to post.
- **Titles and card backs to earn.** Achievements and arena championships unlock honour titles that show on the card
  under your hero's name, and four new card backs. Beat every foe of an arena world to become its Champion. Pick
  them in the card's Style menu; All unlocks shows everything and how to get it.
- **Hidden achievements.** Seven secret achievements that show only a riddle until you find them.
- **Collection.** A new page in the Codex for every class, mount and chapter you have come across, with the gaps.
- **Pause menu.** Esc, the Back key, B/Circle or the new pause button while the wheels are up: resume, spin speed,
  Auto, Options, pause the story or go to the main menu.
- **Charge the spin.** Hold Spin (or the wheel) to wind it up, let go to spin harder. The result is just as random.

### Changed

- **Settings search.** Options has a search box that finds a setting on any tab.
- **Slower if you like.** A Slow spin speed and a Very long auto-play pause, for watching a saga play itself.

## 0.3.0-alpha.5 (2026-11-19)

### Changed

- **Better saga feedback.** "How was this saga?" now also sends what happened in that saga: alignment, fame and
  infamy, guild rank, scars, world events, secret chapters, what came after the ending, and which story options were
  off. Never names or saga text, and you see exactly what is sent before you press Send.

## 0.3.0-alpha.4 (2026-11-19)

### New

- **Secret chapters.** Five very rare chapters that only appear for heroes who fit them: a Necromancer meets the Lich
  King, a Dragon Rider finds the last dragon egg, a time traveller meets their older self, an undead hero is offered
  a cure for death, and a pirate finds the drowned crown. Found ones go into a new Secret chapters page in the Codex,
  which hints at who can find the rest.
- **Alignment.** Mercy, cruelty and what your hero does nudge them towards good or evil, starting from their nature.
  A clearly good or dark hero sees more chapters that suit them, and it shows on the saga sheet and in the recap.
- **World events.** Now and then a famine, a comet, a plague season, a war, the dragon migration or a festival year
  colours the next three chapters. They can be switched off in the story settings.
- **Guild ranks.** Guild members rise from Initiate to Officer through guild work and good deeds in the guild's line
  of business, then fight for the top: Guildmaster, Archmage, Master of Shadows, Grandmaster or Merchant Prince.
- **Ancestor spirits.** Once per saga, an heir who would die is saved by the spirit of the nearest ancestor.
- **A playable retirement.** A retired hero chooses a tavern, students or a memoir, and the wheel says how it goes. A
  student can carry on the family line.

### Changed

- **Mod sounds.** Mods can now replace the music of one scene (title, hero-making, saga, battle) and the victory,
  defeat and legend tunes. The Music Box template in the mod kit has an example of each.

## 0.3.0-alpha.3 (2026-11-18)

### New

- **World Boss.** A new main-menu mode: one boss a week, five in turn: Vaelthrax the Hunger Below, the Clockwork
  Colossus, the Mother of Storms, the Pale Court, and Genesis, the Valkyr of Time. Each has a looping animation.
  Every hero you own can fight once a week, and the real arena odds decide how hard they hit. Players who share
  counts with the world counter (Options > Data) fight one shared health bar together; everyone else keeps their own
  tally.
- **After the ending.** Heroes who ascend, are damned or become undying get one more chapter about what came after.
- **Fame and infamy.** Heroic deeds bring fame; crimes, pacts and conquests bring infamy. A well-known name changes
  the odds on quests, heists and with mentors, and both show in the saga recap.
- **Scars.** Narrow escapes and some lost fights leave a scar, with a small penalty and sometimes a grim bonus. A hero
  carries at most three, on the card and in the recap.
- **Mounts.** A new wheel with 19 mounts, from a steady horse to a griffin or a young drake. A mount helps on quests,
  and a flying or swimming one at sea. A companion can now save the hero from death once.
- **The hero speaks.** The card shows a line in the hero's own voice, chosen by their personality.
- **How was this saga?** After an ending you can say how it went, with an optional note. Nothing is sent unless you
  press Send, and you see exactly what goes.
- **Export your Hall.** The Hall of Legends can save your heroes and statistics as a spreadsheet file.
- **Easy-read text.** Options > Accessibility: a plain, evenly spaced font with more room between letters, words and
  lines. Helps with dyslexia.

### Changed

- **Clearer mod errors.** A broken `mod.json` says the line, the column and a likely fix, and the game tells you when
  a mod could not load.
- **Big screens.** Checked at ultrawide (3440×1440) and 4K: everything fits and scales up.

### Behind the scenes

- The layout check now tests sizes bigger than the screen it runs on.
- A tool turns any GIF or short video into a looping boss animation.

## 0.3.0-alpha.2 (2026-11-17)

### New

- **Old legends return.** Heroes in your Hall of Legends who lived, or cheated death, can turn up in a new saga as a
  mentor, a rival who wants to test you, or a stranger by the fire. You can switch it off in the story options.
- **Player level and card backs.** Every hero, chapter, ending and achievement gives experience (each only once).
  Levels unlock new card backs: Starfield, Rune circle, Gilded lattice, Embers and Void spiral.
- **A Hall worth visiting.** Pin up to five favourite heroes (the first shows on the title screen) and see your
  legends in numbers: average and best rating, your most common races, ranks and endings.
- **Codex and Bestiary.** A book that fills in as you play: the races you have rolled, the worlds you have fought
  in, the endings you have reached, and every foe and dragon with your record against them.
- **Print your hero.** Share > Print sheet makes an A4 page with the front and back at real card size (64 × 96 mm),
  with crop marks.
- **Your own wheel entries, for real.** In the Wheel Workshop, press ✎ on your own entry to give it a description
  and effects: stat changes, a Fate Token, a weakness, a resistance, and a lifespan for races. They are capped so
  they stay fair, and they travel with your hero's codes.
- **A guided first hero.** New players get a few short notes during their first spins.

### Changed

- **More ways to die, and to escape death.** The common deaths and narrow escapes have several versions, and the
  saga recap counts your close calls with death. Story lines about your hero use the presentation you picked.
- **Crowded wheels are easier to read.** On wheels with many slices, like Races, a strip under the wheel names the
  slices around the pointer while it turns.
- **Smoother feel.** Screens and pop-ups fade in, the wheel winds up a little before each spin, and spins no
  longer stutter when the first wheel appears.
- **Tidier screens.** The Spin button sits right under the New Legend settings, and the card's look choices are
  one Style menu.
- **Readable messages.** Pop-up messages are now readable in every theme.

### Fixed

- **Mods can remove and reweight built-in entries**, as the mod kit always said. A mod's `workshop` section was
  checked but never used.

### Behind the scenes

- Story chapters moved into their own files, and translations can now handle plurals and the hero's pronouns,
  ready for translating the story.
- A press kit, a mod gallery with a Share a mod form on GitHub, and a #mods forum on the Discord.
- The Discord bot has /daily, /legend and /versus.

## 0.3.0-alpha.1 (2026-11-17)

The saga update: every saga now ends with a look back at how it went and what became of everything.

### New

- **Saga recap.** At the end of a saga, press **Saga recap** for the whole story at a glance: chapters, years
  lived, your age from start to end, fights won and lost, the closest fight, the biggest upset and the cruellest
  loss, up to eight turning points, your legacy (kingdom, conquests, artifacts, love, heir) and your family line.
- **Epilogues.** A few sentences about what happened after the end: who rules your kingdom, what your heir becomes,
  whether your nemesis is still out there, and loose ends such as an unpaid demon pact. A hero always gets the same
  epilogue, also when opened from a Legend Code. The epilogue is in English for now, like the rest of the story.
- **HD music for Verdant Wild.** The fifth theme has its own seven tracks, like the other themes (placeholders made
  with ACE-Step, an AI music model; see the music credits).

### Fixed

- **Long sagas can be shared again.** Legend Codes are about 12% shorter, and a very long saga no longer fails to
  get a card code: its oldest ordinary lines are left out of the code (never a turning point or the ending), and
  the journal says so.
- **The story journal can be scrolled with the keyboard.**

### Behind the scenes

- Every push is now checked for accessibility, speed (start time, download size, smooth spins), the licences of
  everything that ships, and the balance numbers.
- The Discord bot got a security update.

## 0.2.0-alpha.5.0 (2026-11-16)

A small update: more varied saga endings, and a new opt-in to help find where the game loses people.

### Changed

- **More varied endings.** Retiring used to end half of all sagas. Now the retire chapter more often becomes
  "Passed into legend", sagas run a little longer, and a fulfilled prophecy is likelier to end in legend. At
  Heroic luck: retired 54% to 42%, passed into legend 7% to 15%, deaths 40% to 36%.

### New

- **Share where I stop playing** (Options > Data, off by default): when you close the game, it sends one
  anonymous count of where you were (the screen, or the hero wheel or story chapter). No names, codes or IDs.
  It shows which parts of the game make people leave.

A small fix.

### Fixed

- **The back of the character card**: for heroes with many details, the "Weak to" line was drawn over the saga
  line ("7 chapters · Died"). The details now make room so every line has its own row.

## 0.2.0-alpha.4.7 (2026-11-16)

A small update: send a report when something goes wrong, and phones held sideways fit the spin screen.

### New

- **Send an error report.** When something goes wrong, or the desktop game stopped unexpectedly last time, a small
  note offers to send a report to the developer. You see exactly what it contains first (the game version, your
  platform, the screen, the error and recent log lines, and a note if you write one); home folders and e-mail
  addresses are taken out, and nothing is sent until you press Send. Also in Send feedback; Options > Data can turn
  the offer off. Reports are kept 30 days.

### Fixed

- **Phones held sideways**: SPIN was below the bottom edge on most sideways phones, on both the hero and the story
  screens. The wheel now sits beside the question in a tighter layout, from 568×320 up.
- **The desktop app recovers from a crash** by reloading the game, instead of leaving an empty window.

## 0.2.0-alpha.4.6 (2026-11-16)

A small update that keeps your saves safe as the game changes.

### Changed

- **Saves know which version made them.** When an update changes how something is saved, your save is brought
  up to date the first time you start the game, and an untouched copy is kept first as a backup (Options > Data >
  Backups, marked "before update"). Heroes, sagas and achievements from the very first alpha still load.
- **A save from a newer version is left alone.** If you go back to an older version, it warns you instead of
  changing the save, and importing a newer save file asks you to update the game first.

### Fixed

- **The game tells you when it cannot save.** If this device’s storage is full or the browser blocks it, a
  warning appears once, instead of progress quietly not being kept.

## 0.2.0-alpha.4.5 (2026-11-16)

A small update with fixes from the first alpha tester report on Discord. Thank you, Bobs!

### Changed

- **Fate Tokens say what they are: re-spins.** Wheel results read "+1 Fate Token (re-spin)", the counter says

- **Auto-play waits on each result so you can read it.** Options > General > Auto-play pause: Short (1.5 s, as

### New

- **A tip when you first rule a kingdom**: news arrives between chapters, and a coup can cost you the throne.

### Fixed

- **The first-run guide shows up** even if the HD music question was closed without answering.
- The update banner no longer covers the guide, and tips no longer cover achievement pop-ups.

## 0.2.0-alpha.4 (2026-11-14)

Players told us they didn't know they could remove content they don't want to play, and that the menus were hard to
find their way around. This update puts all of it in one place. The design was tried by 50 test players first; the
results are in [mockups/remodel/test-results.md](mockups/remodel/test-results.md).

### New

- **Content & mods**, on the main menu (it replaces Mods). Three tabs:
  - **What can happen:** one-click presets (Everything, Gentle, Classic fantasy, Custom), the story rules (death,
    aging, nemesis and the rest, moved here from Options), and whole groups of chapters to switch off: Romance and
    heirs, Rivals and betrayal, Kingdoms and war, Demons and prophecy, Curses and plague.
  - **Wheel Workshop**, rebuilt: search, the main wheels first, a checkbox for each entry, **Rarer** and **More
    common** buttons instead of typing numbers, and each entry's chance shown ("Rare · 2.6%").
  - **Packs and mods** together on one page.
- **"Never roll this again"** under a result: two clicks and that race, class or chapter never comes up again. Undo
  it in Content & mods.
- After your first finished saga, a one-time tip shows where to switch things off.

### Changed

- **Options has five tabs instead of seven:** General, Sound, Accessibility, Controls, and Saves and privacy. One
  **Performance** choice (Low, Balanced, High) replaces frame limit, wheel sharpness and glows. Luck stays on the
  home screen; Sound captions are in Accessibility only.
- The in-game ☰ menu has one Content & mods entry instead of Wheel Workshop, Wheel packs and Mods.

### Fixed

- **The Wheel Workshop saves every change.** It used to throw edits away unless you pressed "Save this wheel", so a
  removed race could still come up.
- **Mods keep their state.** A mod switched on showed "Off" again after closing and reopening the Mods screen; the
  switch now says On or Off.
- Races removed in the Workshop are no longer offered under "Choose instead of spinning".

The new text is in English for now; the other languages follow.

A small update that clears up the buttons.

## 0.2.0-alpha.3.3 (2026-11-14)

A small update with a lot of new faces: hero portraits, painted endings, boss tricks and living dynasties.

### New

- **New names in every saga.** Rivals, heirs, houses, kingdoms, cities, ships and the makers of artifacts get names
  made up as you play, in a style that fits their race (elven, dwarven, orcish, infernal and more). A 🎲 button
  next to the hero name suggests one.
- **Card frames by rank.** The card's border grows richer as your hero rises: iron, bronze, silver, gold with
  brackets, glowing gems, and a prismatic frame for a Cosmic Being.
- **Bosses have tricks.** The dragons and the strongest foe of every world each have one: a ward that hides their
  weakness, an immunity, an element they punish, a rage against weaker foes, or a second phase where they rise
  again, stronger. The battle report shows it.
- **Dynasties.** Every house gets a family trait that all its heirs carry, the house remembers each generation,
  and each generation adds renown to the name. **Family tree** on the card shows the whole line.
- **Painted endings.** Every ending has its own illustration on the final screen.
- **Faces for heroes.** Humans, dwarves, orcs, goblins, elves, dark elves, beastkin, halflings, skeletons, lizardfolk
  and gnomes now get a painted portrait on their card when you haven't made one of your own; it follows the
  presentation you chose. Created with Grok.

## 0.2.0-alpha.3.2 (2026-11-14)

A small update: open, collect and fight other players' cards, and share yours with a short code.

### New

- **Open other players' cards.** Paste a card code (or a long Legend Code) in **Open a shared card**: the card opens
  in a viewer, front and back, and your own hero stays as it is. Keep the ones you like in **Collected cards** in
  the Hall of Legends, and pick them as fighters in Versus.
- **Short card codes.** **Get a card code** on your card gives a short code like DU-7K3QXM to share instead of the
  long Legend Code. The first time, it explains what is stored: your card's Legend Code, for 90 days.

## 0.2.0-alpha.3.1 (2026-11-14)

A small update: the trading-card look, and patch notes on Discord.

### New

- **Your card is a trading card.** The front shows your hero's picture, name, type and a line about them; press
  **Turn over** (or click the card) for the back with the rating, every stat, where they're from and what they
  carry. Two fronts: **Arch** (a tarot-style window with a rank seal, the default) and **Full art** (the picture
  fills the card); switch with the Card button, and the game remembers your choice. Every theme colours its own.
- **Save PNG saves both sides** as two pictures, and Share sends both where your device allows it.
- **Patch notes on Discord:** `/patchnotes` shows what's new (pick any version), and release announcements now
  post these notes instead of a list of code changes.

### Fixes

- The back of the card no longer breaks when it's drawn before the last wheel has been spun.

## 0.2.0-alpha.3 (2026-11-14)

You make choices in the story now, your hero's nature changes the odds, battles have painted art, and the
Grimdark pack is built in. Thanks to everyone voting in the Discord poll.

### New

- **Story choices.** At key moments you choose, then the wheel decides how it goes: refuse the demon, pay it or
  hunt for a loophole; fight a dragon, talk to it or rob its hoard; finish a beaten nemesis, spare them or offer
  them a place at your side; show a beaten boss mercy or none.
- **Chapters react to your hero.** Your class, nature, element and past change the odds in heists, hauntings, at
  sea, in training, study, quests and with a mentor, and the wheel says why ("Your Rogue class helps with the

## 0.2.0-alpha.1 (2026-11-13)

The first 0.2 build: a much bigger story, fairer odds, a cleaner spin screen and an optional world counter.

### New

- **Story arcs.** Some chapters start a thread that later chapters pick up: an oracle's prophecy that comes true
  (or that you defy), a demon pact that comes due, an ancient seal that breaks and a world that needs a hero, and
  an heir who grows up to be your ally, your rival or your successor.
- **8 new chapters (49 in all):** defend a city, sail the open sea (watch for the kraken), pull off a heist,
  haunted, meet a mentor, a duel of honour, a war of succession, found a city.
- **6 new endings (10 in all):** died a hero, sealed away, taken by the demon, prophecy fulfilled, risen undead,
  passed into legend. Some endings leave no heir behind.
- **5 new achievements (34 in all)** for the arcs and endings.
- **How your sagas ended**, in Options > Data, for every ending.
- **World counter (optional, off by default).** Switch it on in Options > Data to add anonymous counts to
  everyone's totals: only "a hero was made" or the name of an ending, never names or codes. The totals show there
  and with `/stats` on our Discord.

### Balance

Checked with 10,000 legends and 10,000 sagas at every luck level.

- Sagas run longer: about 12 chapters on average (was 7-8), and early deaths are rarer.
- Fairer fights: on Heroic, arena wins went from 51% to 60% and story battles from 45% to 53%; about 35% of
  sagas end in death (was 48%). Bosses and dragons are matched to your power, and Mortal heroes roll a bit better.
- More variety: Train, Study, Find treasure and Do a quest come up less, curses and exile about half as often, and
  a chapter that repeats gets a smaller slice each time.

### Spin screen

- Everything lines up in one centre column, with the buttons in an even grid.
- Re-spin shows how many Fate Tokens are left. Speed, direction and the token counter moved into
  **Spin settings**.
- The legend panel shows what you've rolled, laid out like a character sheet; **Show upcoming** lists the wheels
  still to come.
- Laptop screens (1366x768, 1280x800, 1600x900) keep the SPIN button on screen, and big monitors use the full
  width with the legend at the right edge. The itch.io player fits without scrolling to SPIN.

### Mods

- The mod kit guide explains the screen layouts ("style, don't move"), and the CSS reference lists the new parts
  (legend rows, Show upcoming, Spin settings).
