DESTINY UNBOUND MOD KIT
=======================

Everything you need to make your own mods for Destiny Unbound: a guide, ready-to-copy templates and the full
reference. You don't need to know how to program. A mod is a folder with a text file (mod.json) and, if you
like, a CSS file, fonts and sounds.

What a mod can do
  - Add new themes: colours, fonts, wheel and card colours, extra CSS.
  - Replace sounds and music, for one theme or for every theme.
  - Add new wheel entries (races, weapons, powers, titles...) with their own buffs and debuffs.
  - Change how likely the built-in entries are.
Mods can't run code, and everything in them is checked when the game loads them.

Try a template in 2 minutes
  1. Open the "templates" folder and pick one, for example 01-colours-only.
  2. Desktop app: in the game open Mods > Open mods folder, and copy the whole template folder into it.
     Then press "Restart to apply".
     Browser: in the game open Mods > Import a mod folder and pick the template folder.
  3. Pick the new theme from the theme button at the top of the screen (or in New Legend > Look).

Make your own
  1. Copy the template closest to what you want and rename the folder.
  2. Open mod.json in any text editor. Change "id" (lowercase letters, numbers and dashes) and "name".
  3. Change the colours, entries or sounds, save, and restart the game (desktop) or import it again (browser).
  4. If something is wrong, the Mods screen lists the problem and the line it is about.

Templates
  00-blank                    The smallest valid mod. Start from scratch.
  01-colours-only             A theme made only of colours. No other files.
  02-styled-theme             A theme with its own CSS and its own font.
  03-sounds                   Replaces every sound and adds music, for all themes.
  04-new-entries              New entries for every wheel, plus changed odds.
  05-wheel-pack               A wheel pack: entries only, one .json file, installed from Menu > Wheel packs.
  06-full-example             Verdant Grove: a complete theme with CSS, sounds and entries.
  07-ember-dominion           The Ember Dominion: a whole grimdark sci-fi setting as a content pack, with its
                              own theme and about forty new entries. Play it, or copy it for your own setting.

Sharing your mod
  Zip the mod's folder and share it. Players unzip it into their Mods folder (desktop) or import the folder
  (browser). Say what your mod contains, and only install mods from people you trust.
