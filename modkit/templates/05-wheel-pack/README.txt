05 - Wheel pack
===============
Sky Pirates: a wheel pack. A pack is a single .json file that only adds entries (and changes odds). It has no
themes or sounds, and it isn't a mod folder.

Installing
  In the game: Menu (the button with three lines at the top) > Wheel packs > Import a pack file, and pick
  sky-pirates.json. Packs can be switched off, exported and deleted from the same screen. They work the same in
  the desktop app and the browser, with no restart.

Making your own
  1. Copy sky-pirates.json and rename it.
  2. Change "name", "author" and "description", then replace the entries. The fields are the same as in
     04-new-entries.
  3. "workshop" changes the odds of built-in entries: "weights" makes them likelier or rarer, "removed" takes
     them off the wheel.

Players can also turn their own Wheel Workshop changes into a pack with Wheel packs > Export my Workshop edits
as a pack.
