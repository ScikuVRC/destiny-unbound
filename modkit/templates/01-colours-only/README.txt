01 - Colours only
=================
Ember Rose: a complete theme made only of colours, starting from Blood Moon. No CSS, fonts or sounds.

What each part changes:
  vars    The page: background, text, boxes, buttons, the wheel's question. See reference/theme-variables.md.
  wheel   The wheel: "pal" are the slice colours (pairs of inner and outer colour, used in turn), "lad" the
          stat-ladder colours from lowest to highest, "glow" and "hit" the light effects.
  card    The character card: row labels ("key"), values ("val"), the box ("panel") and its border ("stroke").

Try changing "--accent" and "glow" first: they change the most for the least work.
Colours can be #rrggbb, rgb(), rgba(), hsl() or hsla().
