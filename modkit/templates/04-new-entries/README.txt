04 - New entries
================
Frontier: one new entry on every wheel (and a second weapon and power), each showing a different kind of buff
or debuff, plus "workshop" changes that make two built-in companions and one weakness likelier.

Each entry
  name   Shown on the wheel. Must not be the same as a built-in entry (reference/wheels.md lists them all).
  w      Slice size. Built-in entries are mostly 2 to 10. Small numbers make strong entries rare.
  dsl    Buffs and debuffs, for example "spd+1 wpn+1 dur-1". See reference/dsl-codes.md.
  perk   A short good line shown under the result.
  flaw   A short bad line.
  art    Races only: how the race looks, for AI portraits.

Wheels: race, birth, bg (background), per (personality), cls (class), enh (enhancement), elem (element),
wpn (weapon), pow (power), weak (weakness), pet (companion), title, mark (mark of fate), hair, eyes, garb (outfit).

Weapons need a quality (q=1 to 5), powers a rarity (rar=1 to 6), titles a tier (tt=1 to 3) and races a lifespan
(life=s, m, l or a). The game adds a default if you leave them out.

If you only want entries and no theme or sounds, a wheel pack (05-wheel-pack) is simpler to share.
