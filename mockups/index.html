FateWheel · 4 mockups

Four visual directions for the character wheel app. Each page shows the spin screen, the final card and the arena with buffs and debuffs, built from the real app components. The app ships all four as switchable themes.

Mockup 1 · The faithful oneClassic Night

Closest to the original videos: pure black, blue-on-dark chip, big white live result, dark-rimmed wheel with the teardrop pointer. Hero screen: the Race wheel and its bonus card.

 Mockup 2 · Ornate fantasyGilded Arcana

Inspired by the gold-framed Season 2 character card. Serif small-caps, gold studs on the rim, double-line panels. Hero screen: the Angel sub-wheel and its perks card.

 Mockup 3 · Game-show energyNeon Arcade

Glowing rim, cyan and magenta accents, pill chip. Built for sharing short clips. Hero screen: a ladder wheel (Speed) showing the race bonus lifting the tier.

 Mockup 4 · Leather and parchmentOld Grimoire

A spell-book look: leather desk, parchment panels, burgundy accent, italic serif result. Hero screen: the Enhancement wheel (half "No enhancement") with its cost.
