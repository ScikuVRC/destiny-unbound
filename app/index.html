How a spin works

  • Bigger slice = likelier. Humans are common, gods are slivers.
  • Tiers shift. A race bonus of +2 lifts what you rolled two steps up the ladder.
  • Buffs and debuffs from race, birthplace, age, height, background, personality, enhancement and weakness. Dashed chips only apply in the right arena.
  • Fate Tokens let you re-spin a result you hate.
  • Then live the story. Spin chapters: quests, dragons, kingdoms, wars, love, heirs. Used chapters grey out; landing on Retire, or dying, ends the saga.
  • At the end an AI image generator of your choice can paint your hero from everything the wheels decided.
