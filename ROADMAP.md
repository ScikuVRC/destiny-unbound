# Destiny Unbound roadmap

What's coming, roughly in order. Destiny Unbound is made by one person in their spare time, so there are no dates:
things land when they're ready, in small updates. Plans change with what players ask for, so tell us what you
want most on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/McDS5HVxzJ) (there's a poll running) or in
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/ScikuVRC/destiny-unbound/issues).

Last updated 2026-11-14, at version 0.2.0-alpha.3.1. What already changed is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Now: the rest of 0.2

- **Real sound and music.** Proper sound effects for the wheel, reveals, battles and endings, and a real track
  per theme, instead of the sounds the game makes up as it plays.
- **Portraits for every hero.** Painted portraits for the most common races, so every card has a face without
  connecting your own image generator.
- **Reroll with consequences.** Fate Tokens that can nudge the wheel one slice, or lock a result before a risky
  re-spin.
- **More Discord commands.** Today's daily challenge, and a Legend Code shown as a card.
- **The story in your language.** The story text in Spanish first, then French, German and Japanese, and a
  native-speaker check of those languages (help very welcome).

## Next: toward the beta

- **More story.** Companions with their own small arcs, a nemesis with a motive who changes as you meet, more
  arcs (a cursed artifact, a war where you pick a side, a plague), a recap of your saga with a short epilogue.
- **The arena grows.** A gauntlet of fights in a row with one life, bosses with their own tricks, best-of-three
  versus and challenge codes.
- **Collecting.** A book of every race, ending, boss and artifact you've seen, card frames by rank, achievement
  badges.
- **Polish.** Readable wheels even with a hundred slices, smoother screen transitions, a guided first hero, a
  pause menu, and a check on real phones, Macs and Linux.
- **Saves that last.** Your Hall of Legends, settings and heroes carried safely into every future version.

## Later

- A fifth painted theme with its own music.
- Weekly themed events and goals you choose for a saga.
- Illustrations for story chapters and endings.
- A place to share mods and wheel packs.
- 1.0, and maybe a Steam release after it.

## How a version becomes 1.0

The game is in **alpha**: everything works from start to finish, but things still change. **Beta** means nothing
big is added any more, only fixes and polish, and anything you make then keeps working in 1.0. The full rules are
in [docs/release-plan.md](docs/release-plan.md).
